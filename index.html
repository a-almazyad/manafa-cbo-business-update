Credit Engine — Live, Waiting, Then Configurable

The engine is launched for Invoice Financing. Expansion now depends on the revised business model.

Three stages
OBJECTIVE

Automate credit assessment consistently across financing products, while keeping every decision explainable and governed by Credit.

01 · DONE

Invoice Financing launched

The first automated scoring model is implemented and operating for Invoice Financing.

Rules · scorecard · thresholds · decision output
02 · WAITING FOR BUSINESS

Revised scoring model

Credit and Business are revisiting the model and incorporating the logic required for other financing products.

Required input · model · product rules · approval
03 · FUTURE

Business-configurable engine

Credit can create and maintain product scorecards, weights and thresholds inside the engine.

Configuration · simulation · validation · monitoring
THE CURRENT GATE Receive the revised scoring model and product-specific logic before continuing expansion.
