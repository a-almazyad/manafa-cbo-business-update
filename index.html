PLATFORM STRATEGY

Platform Strategy

Replacing one growing Admin Portal with clear domains, owners and roadmaps.
02

Why the Current Model Needs to Change

The Admin Portal grew with the business, but the same structure now slows product ownership and delivery.

Current challenges
MINDSET & PRACTICE
  1. Product ownership is unclearWhen most work sits under “Admin,” no team owns the full domain outcome.
  2. Delivery is demand-drivenTeams respond to incoming features instead of managing a product roadmap.
  3. Product measures are limitedSuccess is tracked through delivery rather than adoption, service quality and business impact.
  4. Domain knowledge is dispersedRules and operational decisions are spread across teams and tickets.
SYSTEM & STRUCTURE
  1. Cross-domain couplingA change in one area can create dependencies and regression risk elsewhere.
  2. Permissions are difficult to manageRoles accumulated across unrelated functions in the same portal.
  3. Navigation and onboarding are harderUsers and new team members must understand a broad system before doing focused work.
  4. Scaling creates more complexityAdding products and partners increases the size of the same shared application.
DIRECTIONDefine clear product domains, then move each domain into a focused platform with its own owner, roadmap and measures.
Manafa Product Suite Strategy v2.2
02

How the Strategy Was Developed

The product structure came from internal discovery, external comparison and validation—not from reorganizing the Admin menu.

Discovery process
01INTERNAL DISCOVERY

Stakeholder workshops

Two workshops with internal stakeholders mapped the operating problems, ownership gaps and dependencies created by the current model.

Input: product, business and operations
02EXTERNAL VIEW

Market research & benchmarking

We reviewed how comparable financial platforms separate customer channels, operational domains and shared technology capabilities.

Input: market and competitor structures
03VALIDATION

A/B testing & brainstorming

Alternative structures were tested and refined before defining the target suite, hubs and focused platform products.

Output: target product structure
DISCOVERY OUTPUTA domain-led suite with explicit ownership, modular products and clear separation between audience-facing channels and internal platforms.
Manafa Product Suite Strategy v2.2 · Discovery process
02

The Principles That Guide Us

Four principles determine where capabilities belong and how each product is owned.

Decision principles
01

Clear ownership beats shared ownership

Every domain needs one accountable product owner, a defined outcome and a roadmap.

02

Modular over monolithic

Focused products can change independently without expanding risk across one large Admin Portal.

03

Customer logic stays close to the customer

Borrower, investor, funder and buyer experiences stay in their channels; internal operations stay in domain platforms.

04

Measure what you ship

Each product is measured by adoption, quality, cycle time and business impact—not only release completion.

THE TEST FOR EVERY CAPABILITYWho uses it? Which domain owns it? What outcome does it improve? Can it evolve without coupling unrelated products?
